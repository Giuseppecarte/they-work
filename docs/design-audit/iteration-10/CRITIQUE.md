# Critical review

The closure exercise found an actual release regression which unit counts and
old image evidence had missed. Adding the explicit `storage_process` target made
the manifest invalid inside Docker's dependency-cache layer, even though native
workspace tests passed. The minimal stub correction is verified by the negative
regression, a clean multiarchitecture build and exact-digest executable checks.
It changes build preparation, not the shipped provider behavior.

Recovery is technically preserved but still has a comprehension limitation.
An ordinary failed write leaves the full Not-sent reason and draft visible.
After canonical-state loss, the composer instead shows Observation only; the
draft reappears unchanged after checked supervisor restart and deliberate
reconnection. The PTY proves preservation and zero resend, but the unavailable
view does not explain that preservation. Connections repeats parts of the
recovery diagnostic. These observations belong in the still-open UX-02 study;
they do not justify claiming participant comprehension or inventing a prototype.

The 96 complete composition exports pass route, hit-bound and effective-color
checks. Eight representative full screens were inspected across all six sizes,
both cell geometries and image/light/monochrome modes. At 80×24 the task, current
request, observation and latest result fit together. At 32×14 the emergency
brief clips the title and asks for enlargement to read history, while retaining
a separate Review action. Wide/tall notebooks still have spare space. The other
88 exports are automatically checked, not individually visually approved.

The data evidence is honest about its limits: unavailable provider events, local
retirements and uncorrelated starts use different units. Local history remains
bounded and in memory; a fair retention policy is not permanent storage. Old
requests cannot become current authority through historical replay. Coverage
navigation preserves reading context and cannot execute obscured actions.

PERF-01 now covers the complete planned overlap envelope. The correlation map
stays bounded and paired results remain intact. Large batches still take time:
with 6,400 starts in one phase, debug poll p95 reached 524.72 ms paired and
466.23 ms unpaired. Samples include shared-host contention, and process peak RSS
also includes the measurement harness. This is neither a speed improvement nor
a physical-terminal latency result. The existing frame-cost test also failed
narrowly under concurrent workload before passing in the final full rerun.
The failed evidence stays visible; thresholds and workload were unchanged.

The study preflight required dependencies beyond the portable one-frame smoke.
The isolated archive initially lacked pyte, and adding the entire old dependency
directory shadowed Pillow with an incompatible build. The successful procedure
copies only pyte/wcwidth into the isolated helper location and records their
hashes, pinned Pillow and macOS font. This prevents the smoke's success from being
misrepresented as proof that participant launchers were ready everywhere.

The remaining blockers are material. Cross-compilation cannot demonstrate safe
Windows replacement, and checksum fixtures cannot demonstrate that actual
native CI archives integrate with promotion. Both rows remain open, so REL-02
has not started. Six local engineering closures are useful progress, not a
claim that all S/M work or the application is finished.

Raw terminal text, logs and the captured documentation diff retain their exact
bytes, including blank lines and trailing spaces. The recorded Git whitespace
check therefore flags those evidence files; it finds no authored-source or
Markdown issue. Rust formatting passes separately. Raw RGBA exports are marked
binary locally so textual reviews and checkout line-ending conversion do not
alter compositor bytes.
