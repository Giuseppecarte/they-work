# Critical review of the colorful preview

The material change is visible at both scales. Computer glass, pale bezels,
silver supports, colored upholstery and warm desktops read as separate objects.
Studio, Workshop and Laboratory retain their original construction, while all
four saved palette choices affect desks and team laptops. The room remains
quieter than the people and request labels. The work brief's filled Now tab and
Review request action are substantially easier to locate against navy or cream.

This conclusion comes from fixed synthetic compositor views. It is not evidence
that a new person can identify the correct worker within ten seconds.

## Problems caught and corrected

- The first light-mode lilac lost the text contrast target after xterm-256
  quantization. Its darker replacement passes the truecolor and quantized tests.
- The initial golden refresh missed the sign glyph sheets' background color.
  All 115 changed goldens now preserve their headers, dimensions and symbols;
  only colors differ. The first failed run remains in the evidence.
- The parallel suite exceeded an existing wall-clock rendering budget while
  many render tests ran together. The suite passes serially with the original
  threshold. The parallel failure remains recorded; it is not hidden by a
  weaker assertion or represented as physical-terminal latency.
- The ordinary demo supplies fictional historical questions, not a live
  answerable request. The WSL checklist now distinguishes inspecting those
  records from exercising an exact connected request. The latter remains an
  authenticated trial; synthetic real-UI fixtures test its route separately.
- The first musl preview failed the strict standalone-executable check. Its
  dependency on a musl loader prevented archive delivery. The packaging report
  records that failed attempt and the verified replacement, rather than silently
  distributing a loader-dependent binary under a static label.

## Limits that remain visible

The room's generous floor space and tall narrow views keep their existing
layout. This pass changes material identity, not furnishing density. Compact
tower characters necessarily expose less chair color than the enlarged office.
Pale costumes have less silhouette contrast against light walls; reviewed faces
and raised hands remain readable, and the authored occlusion tests pass.

Attention retains its dense existing list and reading layout. Explicit request
wording, age and coverage warnings remain available; color does not replace
those facts. The image-free view intentionally uses the native roster. The
monochrome view preserves labels, brackets and emphasis, with no claim about
the user's terminal theme or accessibility certification.

The broad compositor register contains 128 checked routes/geometries, with 62
cases visually reviewed through complete individual specimens or full-scene
palette contact sheets. Unreviewed cases remain **not tested** for visual review.
The generated decorative sequence was not reviewed frame by frame; unchanged
motion limits, request interruption and movement geometry are exercised by the
renderer suite.

VS Code/Ubuntu WSL image detection, font rendering, resizing, mouse behavior
and input-to-visible latency are still **not tested in the user's terminal**.
Executable PTYs simulate protocol replies and input; they cannot establish
actual GPU rendering or authenticated-provider correctness. The session record
starts empty so those observations can be recorded honestly.

The local preview does not close REL-02, UX-01/UX-02, native Windows acceptance,
public-release promotion, authenticated sessions or the other publication gates.
