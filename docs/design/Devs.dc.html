THE CAST
nobody is a stock body — every thread is assembled from the wardrobe and keeps that face forever
STABLE, NEVER RANDOM
Each slot is chosen by hashing the thread id, so a worker keeps the same face across restarts and looks identical on your machine and mine. Random-per-launch would make the office unreadable — you recognise people by their hair before you read the name plate.
DETAIL NEVER OVERRIDES SIGNAL
Wardrobe changes cut, not hue: every top stays Claude-orange or Codex-blue. Contractors are the one exception — grey and badged, because “temporary help” outranks which agent spawned them.
SIX SLOTS, NOT SIXTY
head, face, top, desk object, skin, hair colour. Six slots at five or six options give thousands of people from a few dozen sprites — and a floor of five where nobody is a twin.
