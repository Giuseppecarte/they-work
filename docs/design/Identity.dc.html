ONE DESCRIPTION, SIX RENDERERS
A worker’s appearance is a value, derived once from the thread id and carried with them. Every surface asks the same value for what to draw and differs only in scale and projection.

No surface may invent its own artwork. The day the phone gets its own avatar set is the day the ponytail stops meaning Dev 2.
WHAT SURVIVES AT EVERY SIZE
At 96px you see the frames and the ponytail tie. At 16px you see an orange shirt and a brown silhouette with something on the right side of the head.
So the slots are ranked. Shirt hue and hair silhouette must read at 16px and are chosen to differ sharply within one office. Frames, freckles and desk props are luxuries that appear as the sprite grows.
TEST IT, DO NOT TRUST IT
A golden-frame test renders one worker on all six surfaces and asserts the derived look is identical. Consistency by convention lasts until the first person is in a hurry.
A second test builds a full office and asserts no two workers share a silhouette at feed scale — twins are a rendering bug, not bad luck.
