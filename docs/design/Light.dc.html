WHAT LIGHT MODE MOVES
The room lightens: paper floor, chalk walls, ink text. Everything gains weight rather than losing it — light pixel art dies from thin outlines, so strokes stay dark and full.
WHAT IT NEVER MOVES
Shirts stay Claude-orange and Codex-blue. Status stays green, grey, amber, red — darkened just enough to hold contrast on paper. A worker must not read differently because of the time of day.
