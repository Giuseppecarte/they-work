THREE WAYS TO LOOK
same office, same five people, same data — only the camera moves
ISOMETRIC  default
Depth, set dressing and the sign all read at once. The most characterful and the most expensive to draw. Best above roughly 100×30.
TOP-DOWN
A floor plan. Nothing occludes anything, so ten desks fit where isometric fits six. The reading view when the room is full.
SIDE
A cutaway. Faces are largest here, so expressions and props carry furthest — and it survives short terminals better than either other view.
THE CAMERA IS A SETTING, NOT A MODE
All three read the same World and place the same people at the same desks — only the projection differs. Switching cameras never changes what is true, which is why it can be a preference rather than a decision. v cycles them live; the setting only decides where you land.
AUTO
The default. Isometric when the terminal is big enough, top-down when the room is crowded, side when the window is short. It should never be your job to notice.
