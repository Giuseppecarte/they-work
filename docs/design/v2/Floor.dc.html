THE FLOOR
the same room, with people in it you can actually recognise
characters at 4× · one project, five desks
THE ROOM RECEDES, THE PEOPLE COME FORWARD
Walls and plate stay low-contrast so the eye lands on faces and status bars. Furniture is drawn with three flat tones and a dark outline — no gradients, no texture competing with the cast.
STATUS LIVES ON THE PLATE, NOT THE BODY
Name and status bar sit under each desk in a dark chip, so they read against any floor colour and never fight the character's own palette.
DEPTH IS DRAW ORDER, NOT PERSPECTIVE
Desks sort back to front on the tile grid. Nothing scales with distance — a worker at the back is the same size as one at the front, because shrinking them would cost the recognition the cast exists for.
