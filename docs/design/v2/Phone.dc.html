AVATARS ARE THE WORKER, CROPPED
Every avatar here is the same sprite from the floor, cropped to the head. Nobody draws a second set. The day the phone gets its own icons is the day the ponytail stops meaning Dev 2 — and at this size the head alone still carries hair, skin and glasses.
THE CHANNEL IS THE HEADLINE
The big word is the channel you are in, in the channel's own colour, so a glance tells you whether you are reading news or a problem. #blocked carries the count; the others do not shout when there is nothing to say.
ONE PENDING THING, QUOTED EXACTLY
A blocked row shows the command verbatim in amber on black. That string is the entire reason the phone exists: it is what you have to approve, and it must be copyable by eye without opening anything.
WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a chat client. There is no compose box and no reply. It reads, like everything else here. Adding a text field would imply the program can talk back to an agent, which it cannot and should not.
