WHY DERIVED, NEVER RANDOM
Each slot is chosen by hashing the thread id, so a worker keeps the same face across restarts and looks identical on your machine and mine.
Four hair shapes, four faces, three tops, several skin and hair colours give thousands of people from a few dozen parts — and a floor of five where nobody is a twin.
